**Short answer:** Remotion is free if you are an individual, a for-profit
organisation of up to three people, a non-profit, or still evaluating it — even
for commercial work. Organisations of four or more people need a **Company
License**: **$25/month per seat** to make videos by hand (Remotion for
Creators), or **$0.01 per render with a $100/month minimum** to run an
automation (Remotion for Automators). Features are identical on every tier.

Everything below was checked on remotion.dev, remotion.pro and Remotion's
GitHub repository in October 2026. Prices change; the
[License FAQ](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq) is the source of truth.

## At a glance

| Licence | Who it is for | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free License | Individuals, organisations of up to 3 people, non-profits, evaluation | $0 |
| Company License — Remotion for Creators | Organisations of 4+ making videos without an automation | $25/month per seat |
| Company License — Remotion for Automators | Organisations of 4+ running an automation | $0.01 per render, $100/month minimum |
| Both Company options together | — | Combined $100/month minimum |
| Enterprise License | Custom terms, paperwork, private support | Starting at $500/month |

## Who can use Remotion for free

The [Remotion License](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
lists four groups eligible for the Free License:

- an individual
- a for-profit organisation with up to 3 employees
- a non-profit or not-for-profit organisation
- someone evaluating whether Remotion is a good fit and not yet using it
  commercially

The FAQ is explicit about the edges. A single-person company can stay on the
Free License "as long as your total headcount is 3 or less", even if
incorporated. You can make money under it. You can run an automation under it.
And you don't need to sign up anywhere — "simply get started".

The free licence also has no feature limits. The FAQ answers "Does the Free
License have restrictions in functionality?" with a plain "No."

## When you need a Company License

As soon as your organisation has four or more people. remotion.pro describes the
Company License as "Required for collaborations and companies of 4+ people".

The part people get wrong is *who counts*. Remotion's
[Terms and Conditions](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/terms) define headcount as
"all personnel, regardless of whether they use the Remotion Software." So the
FAQ's own example — a 20-person company where only one person uses Remotion —
needs a Company License. The good news in the same answer: on the Creators
option, you only pay for one seat, for the person actually using it.

## Creators or Automators?

The Company License has two options, and you pick by what you do with Remotion,
not by how many people you are.

**Remotion for Creators — $25/month per seat.** For organisations that produce
videos without setting up an automation. The FAQ says it costs "$25/month per
person who writes Remotion code themselves or using agentic coding tools."
remotion.pro describes it as "For low volume manual video creations, and
building motion design systems in a local environment (including using AI
agents and Remotion Studio)."

**Remotion for Automators — $0.01 per render, $100/month minimum.** For
organisations building automations. Developers working on automations do not
need a Creators seat. If you activate both options, a combined minimum of
$100/month applies.

### What counts as an automation

The FAQ defines an automation as "owning code that programmatically calls" one
of a list of APIs and commands. The list includes `renderMedia()`,
`renderStill()`, `renderFrames()`, the Lambda, Cloud Run and Vercel render
functions, `renderMediaOnWeb()`, the `npx remotion render` and `still` CLI
commands — and the `<Player>` component.

That last one surprises people. In the FAQ's words, "organizations building
video editors, prompt-to-video tools, automated video pipelines or using the
Remotion Player will fall under Remotion for Automators."

### What counts as a render

"1 render is the successful generation of a video, audio, GIF, still image or
image sequence." An image sequence counts as one render however many frames it
has. Previews in Remotion Studio or the Remotion Player do not count.

Only Automators pay per render. Free License users and Creators-only customers
never do.

## Enterprise License

An Enterprise License costs the same as a Company License as a baseline, but
with a $500/month minimum instead of $100 — remotion.pro lists it as "Starting
at $500 per month". It adds things that are overhead for Remotion: custom terms
and pricing, signed agreements, compliance and vendor forms, a private Slack,
Discord or Teams channel, a monthly consulting session, and the Editor Starter
included. Purchase orders and vendor onboarding require it.

## Agencies, contractors and clients

The FAQ splits agency work into cases:

- **You deliver only a finished file (an MP4).** The client never gets or runs
  the project, so only your agency's headcount counts. Three people or fewer,
  free; four or more, Company License.
- **The client owns or operates the Remotion project.** Both companies'
  headcounts are added together for the 4-person threshold, and the licence is
  the responsibility of whoever ultimately owns the project's IP.
- **You bring in another agency or contractors** on the same project. Their
  headcount aggregates with yours.

## What you can and can't build

Any commercial use is allowed, with two limits that apply to everyone. You can't
sell Remotion itself, or a derivative of it. And you can't run a service where
users "bring/upload their own Remotion code" for rendering without Remotion's
written approval.

What *is* allowed: a template users personalise and render, a drag-and-drop
editor built on Remotion, and a service that generates Remotion code with an
LLM and renders it — users may even edit that code, as long as your service
generated it first.

## Telemetry and how renders are counted

On the Automators plan it is your job to keep the licensed render count in step
with real usage, adjusted in the remotion.pro dashboard. Client-side rendering
has telemetry enabled by default that cannot be disabled. Server-side rendering
has no automatic telemetry today, but the FAQ says Remotion 5.0 will make it
mandatory for Automators customers. It stays voluntary for Free License users
and Creators.

## Changes coming in Remotion 5.0

The current LICENSE.md opens with a note that the licence "will slightly change"
in Remotion 5.0, linking to
[pull request #3750](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/pull/3750),
which is still open. It lists two effective changes: contractors also count
towards team size, and the Company License becomes bound to Remotion's terms and
conditions. The latest release when we checked was 4.0.537.

## Is Remotion open source?

No — and Remotion says so itself. The FAQ: "Remotion is source-available
software, but it is not open source software according to the Open Source
Initiative's Open Source Definition." If you need an OSI licence, see
[Remotion alternatives](/remotion-alternatives); Revideo, Motion Canvas and
Manim are MIT, HyperFrames is Apache 2.0.

## What this means for third-party components

Libraries built on top of Remotion carry their own licence, but they don't
replace Remotion's. snapcn's free components are MIT and copy into your project
with `npx shadcn@latest add @snapcn/<name>` — you still need a Remotion licence
if your organisation is four or more people. See the
[snapcn license page](/docs/getting-started/license) for what snapcn's own plans
cover.
